PhD Student at Cornell University

Hi I'm Connor, I am a 2nd year PhD student in Statistics at Cornell University. I am grateful to be advised by Ahmed El Alaoui and be supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. I am interested in problems in high dimensional statsitics, statistical physics and computational-statistical gaps.

Currently, I am working on sampling problems in spin glasses: both designing algorithms for lower temperatures and proving computational hardness.
